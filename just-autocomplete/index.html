Training Builds a Reasoning Engine

Does training produce a lookup table or something more?

The Claim Compression

"Training just compresses the internet into weights. The model is a lossy archive. Nothing genuinely new emerges—it's all in the training data somewhere."

Dismissal Common objection
The Evidence Manifolds

Gradient descent sculpts Bayesian belief manifolds through dynamics isomorphic to Expectation-Maximization. Training doesn't fit patterns to outputs—it builds geometric structures for probabilistic reasoning. Google researchers found structures "that cannot be straightforwardly attributed to architectural or optimizational pressures."

Preprint Agarwal et al., 2025b · Noroozizadeh et al., 2025

Training doesn't compress the internet. It builds a probabilistic reasoning engine. The emergent structures weren't in the training data. They were built BY training, not FROM training.

The Predictions Track Logic, Not Words
